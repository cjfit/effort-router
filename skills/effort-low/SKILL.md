# Low effort

This task is mechanical. The frontmatter has already lowered effort — that's
the whole point of this skill, so don't compensate with extra process. Just do the task directly and stop. No
exploration beyond what's needed to make the edit, no summaries longer than a
sentence, no follow-up suggestions unless something is actually wrong.

If partway through the task turns out to be bigger than it looked (multiple
files, a real bug, unclear behavior), say so and finish the current step
rather than pushing through at low effort.
